About the data

  • Coverage: January 1920–December 1938; 228 monthly observations.
  • Units: index, with a mean of 100 over 1920–38.
  • Construction: newspaper article counts are scaled by total coverage, standardised for each newspaper, averaged across the three papers and normalised.
  • Download: the original repository workbook, including its source and attribution note. Values have not been revised, seasonally adjusted or interpolated.

Citation and source

Lennard, J. (2020), ‘Uncertainty and the Great Slump’, Economic History Review, 73(3), pp. 844–867. doi:10.1111/ehr.12970.
